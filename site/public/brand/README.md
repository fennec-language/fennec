# Fennec's brand files

The mark is Scout's head: Fennec's fennec fox, drawn on a 100-unit grid. The SVGs are the masters; every PNG and the ICO are rendered from them.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `fennec-mark.svg` | The mark in colour. |
| `fennec-mark-mono-dark.svg`, `fennec-mark-mono-light.svg` | The mark in one colour, for light and for dark grounds, and for one-colour print. |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png` | The 16 px cut: drawn for the pixel grid, not scaled down. |
| `favicon-mono.svg` | The 16 px cut in one colour. |
| `fennec-app-icon.svg`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` | The app icon: the fox in negative space on an orange squircle. Also the avatar and social square. |

| `fennec-wordmark.svg`, `fennec-wordmark-light.svg` | "fennec" in rounded monoline strokes, whose two n's peak like ears. |
| `fennec-wordmark-display.svg`, `fennec-wordmark-display-light.svg` | A heavy cut of the wordmark, for display sizes only. |
| `fennec-seal.svg`, `fennec-seal-dark.svg` | The seal, for signed releases, download buttons and merch tags. Never below 32 px. |

## Rules

The site's `/brand/` page shows these.

### Clear space and size
- Leave at least a quarter of the mark's width clear on every side, about one ear tip. Nothing goes inside it, text and other marks included.
- Below 32 px, use the 16 px cut (`favicon.svg`), not the full mark scaled down.

### Grounds
The mark's cream ears and face need contrast, so put it only on:
- a solid ground: ink `#0c0b10`, navy `#1d1a36`, cream `#fce3c4` or white;
- orange, with the one-colour cream mark only;
- the quiet part of an illustration, such as the night hero's empty sky, away from Scout and bright detail.

### Never
- The mark or the wordmark on the ear pattern. The ears and face turn muddy against the repeat.
- The mark on a busy part of an illustration.
- The mark stretched, redrawn, outlined or recoloured beyond the one-colour files.

### The pattern
- It's texture: a footer band, a box's lining, a slide background, kept low contrast.
- Keep it away from the mark and the wordmark. When a mark goes on a patterned surface, put a solid panel under it first, with the clear space inside the panel.

### The wordmark and the seal
- The wordmark is always lowercase. The display cut is for hero headlines, banners and the back of a hoodie, never the nav.
- The seal is for trust moments: signed releases, download buttons, merch tags. It doesn't replace the mark.

### Scout
- Scout wears one outfit at a time, matched to the page: Explorer for the tour and the docs, Racer for speed, Courier for kits, Detective for errors, Inspector for checks, Director for replay, Conductor for concurrency.
- Scout heads a page or fills an empty state. He never sits behind text.
- The Scout art is final raster art, made with an image model and then cleaned. Vector redraws of it would be the next step.

### Motion
- In the nav, Scout's right ear twitches and his eyes blink every few seconds, and a cursor blinks after the wordmark on hover. That's all of it, and it stops for anyone who asks for reduced motion.
